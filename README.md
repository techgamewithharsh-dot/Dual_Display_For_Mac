## ✨ The Magic Behind The Speed

**Dual Monitor For Mac** isn't just another screen mirroring app. It was engineered from the ground up by **Harsh Paun** to bypass the latency of traditional Wi-Fi casting (like AirPlay). 

By utilizing a **custom proprietary rendering engine** (VideoToolbox H.264) combined with an **advanced direct-wired USB tunnel** (`usbmuxd`), it achieves something extraordinary: **True Zero-Perceivable Latency**. 

> *"It feels like your iPhone or iPad screen is natively hardwired to your Mac's graphics card."*

## 📥 How to Install & Connect

Because this app utilizes raw Apple system daemons (`usbmuxd`) to achieve its zero-latency USB speeds, the Mac Host app cannot be distributed on the Mac App Store (which requires strict sandboxing). It is split into two halves:

### 1. The Mac Host (Server)
1. Go to the [Releases page](https://github.com/techgamewithharsh-dot/Dual_Display_For_Mac/releases).
2. Download the latest `DualMonitorForMac.dmg`.
3. Open the `.dmg` and drag the app into your **Applications** folder.

### 2. The iOS Client (Display)
1. Download **Dual Monitor For Mac** from the iOS App Store. *(Link coming soon)*

### 3. Start Mirroring!
- Connect your iPhone or iPad to your Mac using a USB cable (or connect to the same Wi-Fi network).
- Open the app on your Mac (look for the icon in your top menu bar).
- Open the app on your iOS device.
- Enter the **4-digit security PIN** displayed on your Mac into your iPhone.
- **Enjoy your ultra-fast secondary display!**
