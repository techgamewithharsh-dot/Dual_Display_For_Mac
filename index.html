How to connect

  1. Download the Mac Host App above and drag it into your Applications folder.
  2. Download the iOS App from the App Store on your iPhone or iPad.
  3. Connect your device to your Mac using a USB cable (recommended for true smoothness) or ensure both are on the same Wi-Fi network.
  4. Open both apps. Select your Mac from the iOS app and you're connected!
